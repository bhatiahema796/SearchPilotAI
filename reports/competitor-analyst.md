## 5. Recommendations for Glow Nest Candle Studio

1. **Create a Candle Safety & Care Hub**
   Develop a comprehensive guide covering candle safety (pets, children, home), burn time optimization, and wax types. Target keywords like "are soy candles safe for cats" and "how to make candles last longer".

2. **Launch a "Gift Guide" Series**
   Produce occasion-based gift guides (e.g., "Best Candles for Housewarming Gifts", "Self-Care Candle Sets for Mom"). Optimize for long-tail gift queries.

3. **Develop DIY Candle Content**
   If Glow Nest offers DIY kits or wants to attract hobbyists, create blog posts and short videos on "How to Make Your First Candle", "Common Candle Making Mistakes", etc.

4. **Implement Structured Data**
   Add FAQPage schema to product pages and blog posts. Create a centralized Q&A page targeting "People Also Ask" questions from candle-related searches.

5. **Enhance Ingredient Transparency**
   Include full ingredient lists and scent notes for each candle. Create a blog post series on "Understanding Candle Wax Types" to establish authority.

6. **Produce Video Content**
   Short-form videos (TikTok/Reels) showing the candle-making process, scent testing, or customer unboxings can improve engagement and AEO signals.
