## 2. SEO Performance Metric Definitions

| Metric Name | Definition | Calculation Formula | Primary Data Source | Target / Benchmark | Current Status (Demo Mode) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic Clicks | Total clicks received from organic (non-paid) search engine results. | Sum of clicks in GSC Performance report for the reporting period. | Google Search Console | To be established after 90-day baseline. | Not Tested — No GSC property connected. |
| Search Impressions | Total times any site page appeared in organic search results. | Sum of impressions in GSC Performance report for the reporting period. | Google Search Console | To be established after 90-day baseline. | Not Tested — No GSC property connected. |
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | Percentage of organic impressions that resulted in a click. | (Organic Clicks ÷ Search Impressions) × 100 | Google Search Console | Industry-dependent; to be set post-launch. | Not Tested — Requires clicks and impressions data. |
| Average Search Position | Mean ranking position of tracked pages/keywords in organic search. | Average position of all queries in GSC, or average of rank-tracker positions. | Google Search Console / Rank tracker | To be defined based on keyword competitiveness. | Not Tested — No keywords or site content to track. |
| Organic Traffic | Website sessions originating from organic search results. | Count of sessions with source/medium = organic in analytics. | GA4 (or equivalent) | To be established post-launch. | Not Tested — No analytics connected. |
| Indexed Pages | Number of site pages stored in a search engine’s index. | Count of valid, indexable pages in GSC Index Coverage / Pages report. | Google Search Console | 100% of indexable canonical pages; exclude thin/duplicate content. | Not Tested — No live site or sitemap to evaluate. |

## 3. AEO Performance Metric Definitions

| Metric Name | Definition | Calculation Formula | Primary Data Source | Target / Benchmark | Current Status (Demo Mode) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI Search Mentions | Number of times the brand or website is mentioned in AI-generated answers across monitored platforms. | Count of brand/entity mentions in AI answer outputs for tracked queries. | AI search monitoring platform / manual audit | To be defined after AEO content deployment. | Not Tested — No AI monitoring active. |
| Website Citations | Number of AI answers that include a hyperlink or reference to the website as a source. | Count of AI-generated answers containing a citation to the client domain. | AI search monitoring platform | To be defined post-implementation. | Not Tested — No AI monitoring active. |
| Answer Visibility | Percentage of tracked AI queries where the brand appears in the generated answer. | (Queries with brand mention ÷ Total tracked queries) × 100 | AI search monitoring platform | To be defined post-baseline. | Not Tested — No AI queries tracked. |
| FAQ Performance | Performance of FAQ-structured content in earning AI answer inclusion or organic rich-result visibility. | Impressions/clicks on FAQ-rich results OR percentage of FAQ queries where the brand answer is surfaced by AI. | Google Search Console (rich results) / AI monitoring | To be defined after FAQ schema implementation. | Not Tested — No FAQ schema or content live. |
| AI Referral Traffic | Sessions originating from AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) that refer users to the website. | Count of sessions with source containing AI platform domains in analytics. | GA4 (custom channel grouping / source-medium) | To be established post-launch. | Not Tested — No analytics connected. |
