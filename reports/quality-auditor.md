## Quality Checks

### 1. Content Quality Standards
- **Originality**: All content must be 100% original and not copied from any source
- **Accuracy**: Product information, ingredients, and candle care instructions must be factually correct
- **Relevance**: Content should directly relate to handmade candles, home fragrance, and related topics
- **Readability**: Content should be written in clear, accessible language suitable for the target audience

### 2. SEO Requirements
- **Keyword Research**: Primary and secondary keywords should be relevant to candle products, home fragrance, and gift items
- **Natural Keyword Usage**: Keywords should be integrated organically without keyword stuffing
- **Meta Elements**: Title tags (50-60 characters) and meta descriptions (150-160 characters) must be compelling and keyword-relevant
- **URL Structure**: URLs should be clean, descriptive, and include target keywords where appropriate

### 3. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Standards
- **FAQ Development**: Questions should reflect genuine customer inquiries about candles, shipping, returns, and customization
- **Concise Answers**: Responses should be clear, direct, and provide complete information
- **Featured Snippet Targeting**: Content should be structured to answer common questions in a snippet-friendly format

### 4. Technical SEO Considerations
- **Mobile Responsiveness**: Website should function properly on mobile devices
- **Page Speed**: Pages should load within acceptable time limits
- **Navigation**: Site structure should be intuitive and user-friendly
- **Schema Markup**: Product and business schema should be implemented where applicable

### 5. Content Structure
- **Headings**: Proper H1-H3 hierarchy with descriptive, keyword-rich headings
- **Paragraph Length**: Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences) for better readability
- **Visual Content**: Alt text for images describing candle products and scenes
- **Internal Linking**: Links to related products, blog posts, and category pages
